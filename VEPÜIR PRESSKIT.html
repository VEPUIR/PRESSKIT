About

VEPÜIR, 23, is a Hardtechno / Industrial and Hardbounce DJ and producer based in Lisbon, Portugal.

His sound fuses hard, driving beats with emotional vocal elements, creating intense and expressive journeys rooted in the underground.

Moving between darkness and emotion, his sets are built on fast tempos, raw energy and moments of vulnerability, designed to connect deeply with the dancefloor and push beyond pure intensity.
